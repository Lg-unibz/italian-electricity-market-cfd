# Data and output rights

The GitHub repository contains no GME or Terna raw files.

The normalized wind input distributed through the companion Zenodo dataset is
derived from the Copernicus Climate Change Service dataset
`sis-energy-global-reanalysis`, DOI `10.24381/3bb607bd`, under CC BY 4.0.

The paper result tables and figures in the companion Zenodo dataset are
released by Lorenzo Gambadori under CC BY 4.0. They must retain attribution to
the research software and to the upstream sources documented in
`data/source_manifest.csv`.

GME workbooks and Terna raw CSV files remain subject to their provider terms
and are not redistributed. Their exact file names, sizes, and SHA-256 digests
are retained in `reproducibility/reference_manifest.json` so independently
obtained copies can be verified.
